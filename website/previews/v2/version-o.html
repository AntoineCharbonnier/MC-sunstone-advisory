About

Critical transaction support.

Sunstone Advisory is an independent transaction advisory firm working with companies, founders, family businesses, investors and boards, in situations where ownership, leadership and capital intersect.

The investor's perspective

Most advisors have seen deals from one side of the table — as a banker who has usually never deployed capital as a principal, or an investor who may have never run a competitive process. We bring both perspectives, combining years of deal execution and private equity experience.

Hands-on involvement

A transaction partner embedded in your team — not a distant intermediary, but a direct counterpart supporting you throughout the process.
